Phone as a 3D window

The front camera follows your eyes and the motion sensors follow the phone, so the screen behaves like a window onto a small room behind it.

  1. Tap Start and allow camera and motion access.
  2. Hold the phone comfortably, then tap Center.
  3. Move your head and tilt the phone.

Everything runs on this device. No video leaves the browser.
